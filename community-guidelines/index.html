Community Guidelines

How to share stories that help other athletes, and what is not allowed.

Last updated October 26, 20262 min read
On this page

The short version

  • Share your own experience, honestly and kindly.
  • Protect other people's privacy. No names, photos or details of teammates, coaches or family without permission.
  • Nothing that could hurt someone who reads it.
  • Every story is read by a moderator before it goes live.

Why we have these guidelines

Stories on Halftime are read by athletes who may be having a hard season. A good story helps the next person feel less alone and gives them something they can try. These guidelines apply to everything you submit, including stories, titles and photos, and they sit alongside our Terms of Use.

Share in a way that helps

  • Tell your own story. Write about what happened to you, how it felt and what helped. Do not post someone else's story as your own.
  • Be honest. Do not make up events or pretend to be someone you are not.
  • Focus on what helped. When you write about a hard time, include what you did next, who you talked to, or what you would tell someone in the same spot.
  • Be kind about others. You can describe a tough coach, teammate or parent, but do not insult or mock them.

Protect other people's privacy

  • Do not use the full names of teammates, coaches, parents or other people. A first initial or a role, like "my coach", is fine.
  • Do not name your school or team in a way that identifies someone else.
  • Only upload photos you took or have permission to share, and do not upload photos where other people can be recognized without their permission.
  • Do not share phone numbers, addresses, social media accounts or other contact details, including your own.

Not allowed on Halftime

  • Bullying, harassment, threats or hate based on who someone is.
  • Sexual content of any kind.
  • Descriptions of methods of self-harm or suicide, or content that encourages them.
  • Tips for extreme dieting, disordered eating or dangerous weight cutting, including specific numbers or routines.
  • Encouraging drug, alcohol or performance-enhancing drug use.
  • Graphic violence or descriptions of injuries meant to shock.
  • Advertising, spam, links to other sites or requests to contact you elsewhere.
  • Anything illegal.

Writing about a hard experience, such as an eating struggle, an injury or a dark time, is allowed. Keep the focus on your feelings and your recovery, and leave out details that could harm a reader.

How moderation works

  • A Halftime moderator reads every story before it is published, usually within 24 hours.
  • We may ask you to change a story, edit it lightly for safety or privacy, or decline it.
  • We may remove published content that breaks these guidelines, and we may suspend accounts that break them repeatedly or seriously.
  • To report a story, email [Contact email] with its title. We review every report.

If you are worried about someone

If a story or message makes you worried that someone is in danger, tell a trusted adult. In the United States, you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or call 911 in an emergency. Elsewhere, call your local emergency number. Halftime moderators do not monitor content in real time and cannot send emergency help.

Need help right now? In the U.S., call or text 988, or call 911 in an emergency.
